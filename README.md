# PROJECT WOW

The umbrella home of four Buildathon projects about invisible losses in the age of rapid AI and data sharing.

The world is evolving rapidly, but rapid AI has brought rapid sharing, and people are going blind about how their data is used. Resources are burnt, privacy is ignored, and most losses are only counted after something goes wrong. PROJECT WOW makes the invisible losses visible.

## The Projects

| Project | What it does | Code | Live preview |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UNSEEN | "Not everything lost is visible." Reveals the invisible losses that shape everyday life: time, food, electricity, water and waiting. | [WOW-UNSEEN](https://github.com/fadilp5x/WOW-UNSEEN) | [Live demo](https://fadilp5x.github.io/WOW-UNSEEN/) |
| TIME TAX | Makes the campus waiting-time tax visible: the hours silently lost standing in queues and waiting on delays. | [TIME-TAX](https://github.com/selmaaaaan/TIME-TAX) | [timetax.ai.studio](https://timetax.ai.studio/) and [run.app](https://remix-time-tax-1806-599355835491.asia-southeast1.run.app) |
| EXHAUST | A forensic mirror for your digital ghost: cinematic privacy forensics and digital exposure analysis for the files you share. | [WOW-EXHAUST](https://github.com/fadilp5x/WOW-EXHAUST) | [wwoexhuast.vercel.app](https://wwoexhuast.vercel.app/) |
| SHADOWPACT | "You vs The App." Shows what an app's terms and conditions actually take from a person, in an animated 3D scene. | [WOW-SHADOWPACT](https://github.com/fadilp5x/WOW-SHADOWPACT) | [shadowpact.netlify.app](https://shadowpact.netlify.app/) |

## About This Repository

This main repository holds the UNSEEN platform site (React, Vite and TypeScript) and the Project WOW dashboard page, the single home that presents all four projects together.
